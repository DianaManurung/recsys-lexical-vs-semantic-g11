E1 & Popularity &

E2 & Biased Matrix Factorization (MF) &
16 configurations: factors $\{32,64\}$, regularization $\{0.02,0.05\}$, learning rate $\{0.005,0.01\}$, and epochs $\{10,20\}$. Fixed item-bias weight $=0$, seed $=42$, and $K=10$. &
Supporting collaborative-filtering baseline. \\

E3 & TF-IDF Content-Based Filtering &
12 configurations: maximum features $\{10{,}000,20{,}000,\text{unlimited}\}$, n-gram range $\{(1,1),(1,2)\}$, and minimum document frequency $\{2,5\}$. Fixed text field: \texttt{content\_text\_basic}; English stop words, sublinear TF, and maximum document frequency $=0.8$. &
Evaluate lexical item representations (RQ1). \\

E4 & Semantic Embedding Content-Based Filtering &
Two configured encoders: \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2} and \texttt{all-mpnet-base-v2}. Maximum sequence length $=256$, batch size $=32$, and input field \texttt{content\_text\_basic}. &
Evaluate semantic item representations (RQ1). \\

E5 & TF-IDF + Popularity Hybrid &
Content weight $\alpha \in \{0.25,0.50,0.75\}$; popularity weight $=1-\alpha$. Component scores are min-max normalized separately per user over unseen warm candidates. &
Optional exploratory analysis of lexical content relevance combined with popularity. \\

E6 & Embedding + Popularity Hybrid &
Semantic content weight $\alpha \in \{0.25,0.50,0.75\}$; popularity weight $=1-\alpha$. Component scores are min-max normalized separately per user over unseen warm candidates. &
Optional exploratory analysis of semantic content relevance combined with popularity. \\

E7 & TF-IDF vs. Embedding by User Activity &
Compare E3 and E4 across low activity ($\leq 5$), medium activity (6--8), and high activity ($\geq 9$) training interactions. &
